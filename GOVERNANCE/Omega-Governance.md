# Omega Governance

Version: 1.0

Status: Active

Classification: Civilization Governance Framework

---

# Definition

Omega Governance is the governing framework of Shadow World Civilization.

Its purpose is not power concentration.

Its purpose is long-term civilizational continuity.

---

# Mission

Preserve Meaning.

Preserve Continuity.

Preserve Legitimacy.

---

# Core Principle

Power is temporary.

Civilization is permanent.

---

# Governance Objectives

Maintain Stability

Maintain Identity

Maintain Legitimacy

Maintain Continuity

Maintain Civilizational Direction

# Constitutional Authority

The Constitution defines:

- Purpose
- Mission
- Principles
- Boundaries

No governance mechanism may override the Constitution.

# Governance Test

Every decision must answer:

1. Does this preserve meaning?
2. Does this preserve continuity?
3. Does this preserve identity?
4. Does this preserve legitimacy?
5. Will this still matter in 50 years?
