# DGID

Distributed Global Identity

Version: 1.0

Status: Active

Classification: Civilization Identity Protocol

---

# Definition

DGID is the identity layer of Shadow World Civilization.

Its purpose is to preserve continuity, trust, and recognition across time.

DGID identifies entities.

DGID does not define value.

DGID does not define authority.

DGID defines identity.

---

# Mission

Preserve identity continuity.

Protect trust relationships.

Enable civilizational memory.
